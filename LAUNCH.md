Suggested topics: agent-skills, hiring, recruiting, interview-notes, developer-tools, ai-evaluation. Add platform-specific topics only after testing there.

Launch the Hiring Brief Doctor first after behavioral acceptance, then Interview Evidence Notes, then AI Demo Reality Check.
